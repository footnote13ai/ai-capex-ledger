    Notes to the ledger

    How it is measured

    Cash gap is operating cash flow minus all-in capital expenditure. All-in capex is cash spent on property and equipment plus assets taken on finance leases, as each company reports them. Data centres taken on operating leases are left out of capex and shown separately, as commitments not yet started.

    Quarters are calendar quarters. Oracle's fiscal quarters end one month earlier (its June–August quarter is shown as Q3); Microsoft's fiscal year ends in June. The CSV carries both labels.

    Groups. Only the Big Five feed the cash charts. Neoclouds (CoreWeave, Nebius, IREN) and chip and hardware suppliers (Nvidia, AMD and others) are recorded alongside them but never added into the totals.

    Credit prices. Credit-default-swap spreads use the latest print from any provider, named on each row. Rows from secondary sources say so in the notes.

    Revisions are logged below and in the repository history. Rows are only ever added; a corrected figure is a new row with the old one kept.
